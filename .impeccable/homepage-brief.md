# Homepage and storefront brief

## Scope and mode

Persuade. Rework the customer-facing TITUN homepage, product discovery, product detail and supporting brand/corporate pages while preserving the existing commerce, admin, inventory, event and payment foundations.

## Audience, job and action

New visitors must understand TITUN and Oshibori within approximately 30 seconds. Consumers should choose a scent and a 25, 50 or 100-piece pack; business buyers should reach the corporate enquiry form without searching.

## Content and constraints

Use the supplied client brief and existing TITUN photography. Do not copy Oshibori Concept. Do not fabricate testimonials, clients, certifications, shipping promises or legal claims. Customer accounts remain undecided.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** TITUN turns a practical refreshment towel into a modern hospitality ritual. The storefront refuses the generic cleaning-product catalogue and leads with the gesture, product material and moment of welcome.

**OWN-WORLD:** A white-led editorial system with near-black typography, restrained antique gold, warm walnut brown and quiet stone surfaces. Newsreader remains the display voice and Manrope the functional voice. Full-bleed hospitality photography, fine rules, square controls and generous whitespace replace bright category colour blocks.

**STORY:** The visitor notices “The Art of Renewal,” understands towels and wipes, learns what TITUN and Oshibori mean, sees benefits and applications, shops the three scents, then purchases a pack or requests a corporate quote.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A slim offer strip and calm navigation sit above a split hero. The hospitality photograph occupies the larger field; the left field carries “The Art of Renewal,” one concise supporting line and two actions. Product packaging remains visible in the scene, making the offer legible without an explanatory card.

**FORM:** Brief-pinned editorial hospitality storefront, code-first for this session. Direction key: `brief-pinned:titun-2026-09-17`. The signature interaction is the pack selector: three precise quantity choices reveal total and per-piece value without promotional noise.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
