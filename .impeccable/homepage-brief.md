# Homepage and storefront brief

## Scope and mode

Persuade. Rework the customer-facing TITUN homepage, product discovery, product detail and supporting brand/corporate pages while preserving the existing commerce, admin, inventory, event and payment foundations.

## Audience, job and action

New visitors must understand TITUN refreshing towels within approximately 30 seconds. Consumers should choose a scent and a 25, 50 or 100-piece pack; business buyers should reach the corporate enquiry form without searching.

## Content and constraints

Use the supplied client brief, reference-site structure and existing TITUN photography. Never place competitor names or language in TITUN content. Do not fabricate testimonials, clients, certifications, shipping promises or legal claims. Customer accounts remain undecided.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** TITUN turns a practical refreshment towel into a modern hospitality ritual. The storefront refuses the generic cleaning-product catalogue and leads with the gesture, product material and moment of welcome.

**OWN-WORLD:** A white-led editorial system with near-black typography, restrained antique gold, warm walnut brown and quiet stone surfaces. Newsreader remains the display voice and Manrope the functional voice. Full-bleed hospitality photography, fine rules, square controls and generous whitespace replace bright category colour blocks.

**STORY:** The visitor notices “The Art of Renewal,” sees the product immediately, shops the three scents, understands the product details and applications, then purchases a pack or requests a corporate quote.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A slim offer strip and two-level commerce navigation sit above a compact split hero. The product and packaging occupy the larger field; the left field carries “The Art of Renewal,” verified product facts and two actions. The visitor reaches the shoppable scent collection immediately after the hero.

**FORM:** Brief-pinned editorial hospitality storefront, code-first for this session. Direction key: `brief-pinned:titun-2026-09-17`. The signature interaction is the pack selector: three precise quantity choices reveal total and per-piece value without promotional noise.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
