# Events journal brief

## Scope and mode

Read and persuade. Build a dedicated, date-aware TITUN Events Journal and archive, with individual story pages and CMS-authored attendance paths. This is a new events hub, not a renamed Community page.

## Audience, job and action

Visitors arriving from mobile and social channels should browse all, upcoming or past TITUN experiences; understand each gathering through imagery and concise editorial copy; then read, register or purchase a ticket where relevant.

## Content and constraints

Use only administrator-authored event stories, dates, venues, links, pricing and supplied TITUN media. Do not fabricate past events, speakers, testimonials or attendance claims. Past events remain in the archive after their date passes. The Le Luxe journal index at `https://leluxe.co/blogs/our-blog` is the browsing-quality reference, translated into TITUN’s established visual system rather than copied literally.
