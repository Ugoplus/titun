# Community journal brief

## Scope and mode

Read and persuade. Rework the customer-facing Community index and event detail pages as one editorial event journal while preserving the existing event CMS, free registration, paid checkout, recommendations, capacity and email foundations.

## Audience, job and action

Visitors arriving from mobile and social channels should understand the story behind a gathering, determine whether it is free or paid and reserve a place without moving into a separate event system.

## Content and constraints

Use only administrator-authored event stories, dates, venues, pricing and supplied TITUN photography. Keep “Read the story” and “Attend the event” attached to the same event. Do not fabricate past events, speakers, testimonials or attendance claims. The Le Luxe journal index at `https://leluxe.co/blogs/our-blog` is the composition quality bar, translated into TITUN’s established visual system rather than copied literally.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** TITUN gatherings are remembered as stories before they are bought as tickets. The Community experience behaves like an editorial journal whose natural conclusion is attendance.

**OWN-WORLD:** Preserve The Quiet Ritual: porcelain white, ritual ink, warm walnut, restrained gold, Newsreader display type, Manrope operational type, square controls, fine rules and real hospitality photography. Generous white space and offset paper-like story sheets carry the journal character.

**STORY:** The visitor enters a quiet journal index, scans image-led event stories, opens one story, reads the full account and reaches its attendance section in the same uninterrupted page. Free events register directly; paid events continue through checkout.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A centered TITUN Journal masthead establishes the reading mode before a two-column desktop story grid. Each 4:3 image leads into an offset white text sheet containing date, admission, venue, excerpt and both story and attendance actions. Mobile becomes one linear, fully contained column.

**FORM:** User-pinned editorial journal, code-led. Direction key: `brief-pinned:leluxe-community-2026-09-20`. The signature composition is the image-to-offset-story-sheet handoff; the signature interaction is the event card’s paired reading and attendance path.

**FINISH:** The build is incomplete until 1440px and 390px index and detail captures show no clipping, all operational controls remain usable, the detector’s mechanical findings are addressed and the finish reviewer returns a shippable verdict.
